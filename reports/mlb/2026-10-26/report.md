## Not covered

Starting pitchers, bullpen usage, park factors, weather and injuries are **not** in this report — and in baseball the starting pitcher is the single largest per-game factor there is, larger than anything the team ratings above can capture.

That is a limit of the source, not an oversight: the feed carries final scores only. A projection built from team run rates is a real signal, but it is a team-level one, so treat a game with an ace against a bullpen day as under-modelled here. See the README.
